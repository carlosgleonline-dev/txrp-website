More than a server. A county that runs like the real thing.

Texas State Roleplay was founded by Daniel on Christmas Eve 2023 with one goal: give ER:LC players a place where roleplay is taken seriously and the community still feels like home. Today, more than 26,000 members call Collin County their beat.

Every department has its own leadership, training and chain of command. Calls get real responses, scenes get worked properly, and staff are there to keep it fair. You can start the day you join as a Melissa police officer or volunteer firefighter, then work your way up to DPS, the Sheriff's Office, or the Marshals.

    Starting a priority

    To start a priority, use /priority request in the commands channel.

    • Your priority must include 2 or more people to be accepted.
    • Give a detailed backstory. We encourage creativity over basic roleplays.
    • When it's accepted, you'll get a direct message with a Start button. Press Start when you're ready to begin.

    Guidelines

    Restricted roleplays

    Make sure your priority isn't in our Restricted Roleplays section. If it is, your request will be denied and moderation action may apply to the requester.

    One in-game life

    Your priority lasts for 1 in-game life. Once you die in roleplay, it's over. Contact staff so the priority is properly stopped.

    After evading a pursuit

    Once you evade law enforcement, find somewhere to hide. Realistically, you wouldn't evade police just to break more laws in plain sight. Hide and call a moderator to end your priority.

    Harming another member

    Simply killing another member doesn't need a priority. Use common sense: harming another member can still be handled as passive roleplay.

    Skip the in-game queue

    Exclusive to Server Boosters. Use the Queue Bypass button in our Discord to jump the line.

    1. Join any public server.
    2. Click the Queue Bypass button in the Discord.
    3. Press Next to confirm your queue bypass.
    4. Join the in-game queue within 10 seconds.

    Requirements

    • You are a Server Booster.
    • You are verified through the proper account using Melonly.
    • The in-game queue is greater than one.
    If you're already in-game, you can't use the Booster Queue.
